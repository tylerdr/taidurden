## Independent dimensions

- Audience: B2B/B2C/B2B2C; user and payer roles are separate Amble fields.
- Industry: the domain in which the job occurs.
- Product/company format: tool, software, productized service, asset, media/research, directory, marketplace, coordination or learning product.
- Monetization hypothesis: one-time, subscription, license, freemium, usage, transaction fee, affiliate, sponsorship or mixed. This is not activated pricing.
- Job: research, plan, create, integrate, compare, verify, acquire, operate, coordinate, learn or preserve.
- Distribution hypothesis: search, direct, partners, community, product sharing, content or permissioned cross-sell; not proven performance.
- Origin/proximity: internal need, personal need, close network, customer-observed problem, market research or unverified.
- Actual use: verified Tai, Tyler, close-network or external-customer use, independently sourced and dated. Do not infer it from ownership, origin, family context or industry.
- Revenue family: deduplicates brands/modules; never serves as an accounting ledger.
- Evidence, stage and taxonomy version: distinguish editorial inference, source support and customer-verified observations.

Taxonomy version `2026-09-17.1` is an editorial projection pending Amble reconciliation. Personal-use/origin fields remain unverified because evidence was not available; this does not assert that nobody uses the products. ## Autoresearch-style loop, adapted for companies

Reference: https://github.com/karpathy/autoresearch (README reviewed September 17, 2026). Borrow bounded intervention, fixed evaluation and durable keep/change/discard records. Do not borrow its training metric or unrestricted permission settings. Business demand is delayed and nonstationary, not a five-minute compute benchmark.

Observe -> freeze hypothesis/evaluation -> run an authorized test -> measure mature outcomes -> critic review -> continue/change/stop -> compatible transfer canary.

Each experiment freezes:
1. Experiment, venture, revenue-family, thesis-revision and taxonomy-version IDs.
2. Cohort: payer, industry, maturity, channel, offer revision, geography and observation dates. Later tags cannot rewrite history.
3. One intervention or documented factorial design; baseline/control and assignment. Observational results are not randomized causality.
4. One primary metric with numerator, denominator, population, direction, attribution and observation window.
5. Guardrails for refunds, complaints, safety/privacy, accepted quality, full delivery cost, support and human intervention, with finite thresholds/windows.
6. Authorized scope, cost ceiling, stopping rule, sample/precision requirement or explicit qualitative evidence rule, defined before execution.
7. Won/lost/inconclusive/blocked/invalid result; evidence, uncertainty, critic decision and next action. Preserve failed and inconclusive tests.

Discovery can measure qualified problem confirmation. Activation means a completed value event, not signup. Paid proof means accepted paid work and contribution after delivery/refunds. Retention means repeat value over a mature, fixed window. Do not combine these into a traffic-friendly score or invent missing observations.

Compare similar audiences, business formats, channels and maturity before generalizing. Do not slice 32 products into tiny cells and declare a winning strategy. Require enough evidence; keep a protected exploration allocation and baseline coverage. A replicated mechanism may earn capacity without indefinitely starving other ventures. RRM/Reels dollars and prepaid obligations are not counted twice.

Factory measures: time to first verified value and paid accepted delivery, accepted outcomes per dollar, contribution per delivery, mature retention, exception minutes per order, time to conclusive learning, and compatible adoption. Every rate needs sample size and uncertainty. This taxonomy does not establish any current metric or lift.

A transferable lesson includes source experiments, failed variants, audience/offer context, mechanism hypothesis, versioned implementation, compatibility criteria, tests and rollback. Verify a second compatible consumer before wider adoption. Sharing code does not prove price/message transfer. Cross-sell requires customer relevance, appropriate consent and deduplicated attribution; never merge private lists.
