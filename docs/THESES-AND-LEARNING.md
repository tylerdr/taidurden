## Launch-learning contract

Observe -> freeze evaluation -> authorized bounded intervention -> measure mature outcomes -> critic review -> continue/change/stop -> compatible transfer.

Freeze the entire membership set, taxonomy version, accepted thesis revision, offer/release, audience/payer, channel, geography, maturity and attribution window before assignment. New labels must not silently rewrite history. Overlapping memberships do not create independent replications.

Predefine one primary metric with numerator, denominator, baseline/comparator, direction and observation window; finite cost and authority; quality/refund/support/safety/privacy guardrails; and stopping/sample/precision rules or explicitly qualitative evidence criteria. Preserve unsuccessful, inconclusive, blocked and invalid outcomes. Signup is not activation; accepted value is. Paid proof includes full delivery cost and obligations.

Compare comparable cohorts; a shared thesis alone does not make a health directory and roofing service comparable. Do not infer causality from observational tag slices, treat tiny samples as winners, or remove baseline coverage from other ventures. Negative results remain evidence.

Transfer packages need sources, failed variants, limitations, a mechanism hypothesis, compatible consumers, a versioned implementation, tests and rollback. Verify a second consumer before broad rollout. Cross-sell requires relevance, permission and attribution; never pool private customer data or mailing lists.

Measure time to verified value, accepted outcomes, contribution, mature retention, full cost, repeated exceptions and adopted improvements. Owner time returned is not inferred from deploys. This release installs no new experiment runner and establishes no revenue or freedom metrics.
